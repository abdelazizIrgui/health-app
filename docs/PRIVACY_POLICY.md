# Privacy Policy — Health Tracker

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

Health Tracker is a private period (menstrual cycle) tracker. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **your data stays on your phone**.

## What the app stores

The app stores the following **only on your device**:

- Your profile: name, email address, phone number and date of birth, as you entered them.
- Your answers to the setup questions.
- Your logged periods, flow, symptoms and mood.
- Your settings (language, reminders).
- If you turn on the app lock: a **hash** of your PIN (not the PIN itself) in your phone's secure storage.

## What the app does not do

- It has **no server** and no user account. Nothing you enter is sent to the developer.
- It has **no analytics, no advertising and no tracking**.
- It does **not sell or share** your data with anyone.
- It does not use your location, contacts, photos or microphone.

## Things you choose to do

- **Backup / export**: when you use "Export", the app creates a text with your data and opens your phone's share sheet. It goes only where **you** send it (for example to a note or a message). Keep it somewhere safe.
- **Doctor report (PDF)**: the app creates a PDF on your phone and opens the share sheet. It goes only where you send it. It contains your health information, so share it only with people you trust.
- **Reminders**: notifications are created **locally on your phone**. No server sends them. Their wording is kept discreet because notifications can appear on a locked screen.
- **Fingerprint / face unlock**: handled by your phone. The app never receives or stores your fingerprint or face data.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings → Delete all my data** removes everything the app stored, including the app lock and reminders.
- Uninstalling the app also removes its data from your phone.
- Because the data exists only on your device, the developer cannot access, recover or delete it for you. If you forget your PIN, the only way back in is to erase the app's data (you can restore a backup you made earlier).

## Not medical advice

Predictions (next period, fertile window) are **estimates** based on what you log. They are not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a method of contraception. The "worth mentioning to a doctor" notes are only suggestions. If you are worried about your health, talk to a doctor.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, because it sends no information off the device. If a young person uses the app, a parent or guardian may help them with the setup.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
