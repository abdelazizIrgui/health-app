# Privacy Policy — Health Tracker

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

Health Tracker is a private period (menstrual cycle) tracker. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **your health data stays on your phone**, except for the optional chat assistant, which is explained below.

## What the app stores

The app stores the following **only on your device**:

- Your profile: name, email address, phone number and date of birth, as you entered them.
- Your answers to the setup questions.
- Your logged periods, flow, symptoms and mood.
- Your settings (language, reminders) and the list of reminders you received.
- If you turn on the app lock: a **hash** of your PIN (not the PIN itself) in your phone's secure storage.

## What the app does not do

- It has **no user account** and no online database. Your profile, periods and logs are never uploaded.
- It has **no analytics, no advertising and no tracking**.
- It does **not sell** your data.
- It does not use your location, contacts, photos or microphone.

## The chat assistant (optional)

The app has a chat assistant that answers general questions about the menstrual cycle. **It is the only feature that sends information over the internet**, and only after you agree on the first screen and only when you send a message.

- **What is sent:** the messages you write in the chat (the recent conversation). If you choose "Use my cycle day and phase", the app also sends your current cycle day, your usual cycle length, your current phase and whether you are on your period. You can turn this off on the agreement screen.
- **What is never sent:** your name, email, phone number, date of birth, exact dates, or your logged periods and symptoms.
- **Where it goes:** to a small server program run by the developer on Cloudflare, which passes the conversation to Cloudflare's AI service (Workers AI) to write the answer. The developer does not store the conversation. According to Cloudflare's documentation, Cloudflare does not use this content to train its AI models. Cloudflare may keep technical records under its own terms.
- **Please do not write anything you do not want to share.** Do not write personal identifiers or details that could identify you.
- The conversation is kept only while the chat screen is open and is not saved on your phone.
- The assistant gives **general information, not medical advice**.

## Things you choose to do

- **Backup / export**: when you use "Export", the app creates a text with your data and opens your phone's share sheet. It goes only where **you** send it. Keep it somewhere safe.
- **Doctor report (PDF)**: the app creates a PDF on your phone and opens the share sheet. It goes only where you send it. It contains your health information, so share it only with people you trust.
- **Reminders**: notifications are created **locally on your phone**. No server sends them. Their wording is kept discreet because notifications can appear on a locked screen.
- **Fingerprint / face unlock**: handled by your phone. The app never receives or stores your fingerprint or face data.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings → Delete all my data** removes everything the app stored on your phone, including the app lock, the reminders and your chat agreement.
- Uninstalling the app also removes its data from your phone.
- Because your health data exists only on your device, the developer cannot access, recover or delete it for you. If you forget your PIN, the only way back in is to erase the app's data (you can restore a backup you made earlier).

## Not medical advice

Predictions (next period, fertile window) are **estimates** based on what you log. They are not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a method of contraception. The "worth mentioning to a doctor" notes and the chat assistant's answers are only general information. If you are worried about your health, talk to a doctor.

## Children

The app does not create accounts and does not upload your profile or logs. If a young person uses the app, a parent or guardian may help them with the setup, and should read the chat assistant section above.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
